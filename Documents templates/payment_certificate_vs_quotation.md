# Construction & Project Controls Concepts
## Quotation vs. Payment Certificate

This document serves as a reference for agents and engineers regarding the distinction between a Quotation (عرض سعر / مقايسة تقديرية) and a Payment Certificate (مستخلص أعمال).

### 1. The Quotation (عرض السعر / المقايسة التقديرية)
**What it is:** A Quotation is a formal **estimate or offer** submitted by a contractor or supplier to a client **before** any work begins. 
*   **Purpose:** To agree on the scope of work, the unit prices, and the estimated total cost of the project. It forms the financial basis of the contract.
*   **Quantities:** The quantities listed in a quotation are usually **theoretical or estimated** (كميات تقديرية) based on architectural drawings or initial site visits.
*   **Timing:** It is created at the very beginning of the project lifecycle (Bidding/Contracting phase).

### 2. The Payment Certificate (مستخلص الأعمال)
**What it is:** A Payment Certificate is a **billing document** (an invoice for construction/projects) submitted by the contractor **during or after** the execution of the work. 
*   **Purpose:** To officially request payment for the work that has *actually been completed* up to a specific date. 
*   **Quantities:** The quantities in a payment certificate are **actual, measured quantities** (كميات منفذة على الطبيعة) verified by the site engineer or consultant.
*   **Structure:** It tracks progress dynamically using three distinct quantity columns for every item:
    1.  **Previous (سابق):** Work billed in all previous certificates.
    2.  **Current (لاحق / حالي):** Work completed since the last certificate.
    3.  **Total to Date (كلي):** Previous + Current.
*   **Timing:** It is created periodically (e.g., monthly) during the project (مستخلص جاري / Running Certificate) and once at the very end (مستخلص ختامي / Final Certificate).

---

### 3. Quick Comparison Cheat Sheet

| Feature | Quotation (عرض السعر) | Payment Certificate (مستخلص) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **When is it used?** | **Before** work starts. | **During/After** work is done. |
| **What does it say?** | "Here is what we *plan* to do, and how much it *should* cost." | "Here is what we *actually* did, please pay us for it." |
| **Quantities** | Estimated / Planned. | Actual / Measured on-site. |
| **Tracking** | Static (just lists the total expected quantity). | Dynamic (Tracks Previous, Current, and Total quantities). |
| **Financial Outcome** | Establishes the **Contract Value** (Total Budget). | Calculates the **Net Payable Amount** (Amount due *minus* advance payments or previous deductions). |

**Summary Rule of Thumb:** 
The **Quotation** is the *promise* and the *price list*. 
The **Payment Certificate** is the *proof of work* and the *bill*.
